This policy explains what data the App handles when a Shopify merchant ("Merchant") installs it,

data is used.

## Data we collect

**From the Merchant's store, through Shopify's API:**
- Order details needed to prepare WhatsApp messages: order number, total, currency, item names and quantities, payment method (to detect Cash on Delivery), order status page link and tracking links.
- Customer first name and phone number, for orders and abandoned checkouts.
- Whether the customer ticked the "Send me order updates on WhatsApp" box.
- The Merchant's store name, WhatsApp number and app settings.

**From the storefront widgets:** daily counts of clicks per widget type. No personal data, cookies or IP addresses are stored.

**From the order confirmation page:** whether the customer confirmed or cancelled their order, and when.

We do **not** collect payment card details, passwords, email addresses or postal addresses.

## How we use it
- To show the Merchant ready-to-send WhatsApp messages and the status of each order.
- To tag orders in the Merchant's Shopify store as confirmed or cancelled.
- To show the Merchant simple statistics.

We do not send WhatsApp messages ourselves. Messages are sent by the Merchant from their own WhatsApp account.
We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Sharing
We share data only with service providers needed to run the App: [hosting provider, e.g. Render, region],
[database provider], PostHog (product analytics and error logs about how merchants use the App;
customer names, phone numbers, emails and addresses are removed before anything is sent), and, only when a merchant presses a translate button, Google Cloud Translation, which
receives the widget or message-template text being translated (merchant-written text, never customer data).
We will disclose data if the law requires it.

## Retention
- Order and checkout records: kept while the App is installed; checkouts older than [30] days are deleted.
- When a Merchant uninstalls the App, all their data is deleted within 48 hours of Shopify's `shop/redact` request.
- When Shopify sends a customer redaction request, we delete that customer's records.
